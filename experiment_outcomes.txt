Question: What is the full meaning of RAG?

Answer:
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an advanced hybrid model architecture that combines retrieval mechanisms with generative language models to enhance the accuracy of output generation, addressing limitations of large language models like LLMs. It involves integrating a retrieval component that retrieves relevant information from external sources and a generation component that processes this information to produce human-like text. RAG models aim to improve upon traditional LLMs by incorporating real-world data and updating their knowledge based on evolving contexts.

Question: What are the three principal contributions of Adaptive-RAG?

Answer:
The three principal contributions of Adaptive-RAG are:

1. It proposes the Adaptive Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework, referred to as Adaptive-RAG, designed to handle queries of various complexities.
2. It dynamically adjusts its query handling strategies based on the complexity of queries encountered, spanning from the most straightforward to the most complex queries.
3. It leverages a smaller language model with query-complexity pairs, automatically annotated from the predicted outcomes and inductive biases in datasets, to select the most suitable strategy for answering each query. This allows for efficient allocation of resources to handle complex queries while effectively managing simpler ones. 

These contributions aim to enhance the overall accuracy and efficiency of QA systems, providing a robust middle ground among iterative LLM augmentation methods for complex queries, single-step methods for simpler queries, and even no-retrieval-augmented methods for the most straightforward queries.
